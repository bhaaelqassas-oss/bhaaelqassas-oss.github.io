Services

  • Echocardiography (heart ultrasound)
  • ECG (electrocardiogram)
  • 24 and 48-hour Holter ECG monitoring
  • 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure monitoring
  • Diagnostic cardiac catheterization and coronary stenting
  • Management of high blood pressure and cholesterol

When to see a cardiologist

  • Chest pain or tightness, especially with effort
  • Shortness of breath or unusual fatigue
  • Palpitations or an irregular heartbeat
  • High blood pressure or high cholesterol
  • Follow-up after a stent, heart attack or heart surgery
  • A family history of heart disease

Common questions

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book a consultation directly.

What should I bring?

Any previous ECGs, echo reports, lab results and a list of your current medications.

Is echocardiography done at the clinic?

Yes. Echo and ECG are done at the clinic, usually during the same visit when needed.
